## 5. Signature Move

A bespoke HTML5 Canvas synthesizer rendered in the DPI Engine exhibit. Interactive hover allows inspecting packet payload headers and throughput diagnostics directly.

## 7. Structure & Grammar Choice
- **Chosen Grammar:** **Curated Academic Gallery / Exhibition** (`uniqueness.md` §2.6) with **Standing Hero Layered Depth** (`hero-depth.md`).
- **Why other seven grammars lost:**
  - *Filmic one-shot:* Disqualified as the default trap that prior builds fell into; an academic leader is not a single linear consumer pitch.
  - *Live surface:* Disqualified because Dr. Akram is an academic and university leader, not a SaaS console.
  - *Continuous world:* Disqualified; continuous camera chain is fragile and forbidden unless the subject is literally a physical geographic flight.
  - *Typographic poster:* Disqualified; Dr. Akram has extensive authentic photographic documentation (campus architecture, EV prototypes, lab awards) that must be visible.
  - *Chaptered editorial:* Close second, but pure editorial forbids horizontal object rails and physical museum cataloging.
  - *Split stage:* Disqualified; comparing two halves doesn't fit a unified 30-year academic career.
  - *Cutlist:* Disqualified; rapid staccato cuts destroy academic gravitas.
